How this tracker works

This page lists qualifying job postings (Product Manager, Technical Program/Digital Transformation Manager, Strategy Consultant, QA/Test Automation Manager) scored High → Low on interview likelihood. New postings are added over time by editing the jobs-db JSON block in this file's source and re-deploying — nothing is removed automatically. Each posting shows whether its "within the last 24 hours" posting date could be independently confirmed or is unverified. Use the buttons on each row to mark a posting Interested, Applied, or Ignored — this status is saved in your browser's local storage, so it's private to your device/browser and survives page updates, but won't sync across devices.
