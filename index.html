A running list of postings scored and ranked for fit against 8 years across product, technical delivery, strategy consulting, and QA — refreshed as new roles are found, nothing dropped once it's tracked.

How this tracker works

Every update searches LinkedIn/Indeed/Glassdoor and general job boards for postings matching the target role families, locations, and seniority band, scores each one High → Low on interview likelihood, and adds new qualifying postings below — nothing already tracked is deleted. Each posting shows whether its "within the last 24 hours" posting date could be independently confirmed or is unverified. Use the buttons on each card to mark a posting Interested, Applied, or Ignored — saved in your browser's local storage, so it survives every refresh but is private to this device.
