A running list of postings scored and ranked for fit against 8 years across product, technical delivery, strategy consulting, and QA — refreshed as new roles are found, nothing dropped once it's tracked.

How this tracker works

Every update searches LinkedIn/Indeed/Glassdoor and general job boards for postings matching the target role families, locations, and seniority band (posted within the last 7 days), scores each one High → Low on interview likelihood, and adds new qualifying postings below. A posting is only kept if its date and active/not-expired status could be independently confirmed by opening the actual listing — otherwise it's marked unverified or dropped. Use the buttons on each card to mark a posting Interested, Applied, or Ignored — saved in your browser's local storage, so it survives every refresh but is private to this device.
